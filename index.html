THE TECHNOLOGY

Reading biology
one molecule at a time.

A solid-state nanopore turns molecular passage into an electrical signal — creating a new way to study the building blocks of biology.

HOW A SOLID-STATE NANOPORE WORKSANIMATED PRINCIPLE
Moving biomolecule Ionic flow Current blockade
01

A tiny opening in a membrane

A nanopore is formed in a thin solid-state membrane, creating a nanoscale pathway between two electrolyte chambers.

02

Molecules change the ion flow

As a molecule moves through or interacts with the pore, it can disturb the flow of ions through the opening.

03

Electrical events become data

The readout system measures changes in ionic current. Software can then help process and visualize those signal events.

Illustrative principle only. Actual signal characteristics depend on pore geometry, analyte, electrolyte, surface chemistry and operating conditions.
